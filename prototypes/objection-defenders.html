Objection Defenders

Objections are falling from the sky. Fly the ship, blast them out of the air, and keep your team's pipeline alive. Your gun runs on good answers: when the ammo runs dry, a buyer objection appears and only the right response reloads you.

Fly & fire

← → to steer, Space to shoot. On a phone, drag to move and tap FIRE. Big rocks take three hits and pay the most.

Reload = a real objection

Ten shots per clip. Empty clip: a buyer line appears with four responses. The one that acknowledges and asks refills you. Argue, cave, or dodge and you lose a life.

Boss rocks

Clear a wave and a boss rock descends carrying a stacked objection. Bullets bounce off it. Only your own words work: type the rebuttal, Sales Coach AI grades it.

Five waves: Price, Timing, Trust, Competition, Authority. Three lives. A rock that reaches your ship costs one. Heat multiplier climbs with every right answer.
