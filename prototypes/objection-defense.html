Objection Defense

Objections come down the screen. Shoot them with the right response before they reach you. Every wave ends with a boss objection you have to answer in your own words — Sales Coach AI grades it live.

Pick the move

Four responses. One acknowledges and asks. The others argue, cave, or dodge. Pick fast — closer objections score less.

Build heat

Right answers stack a multiplier up to ×3. A wrong pick marks the option and drops the objection closer. Three hits and the call's over.

Beat the boss

A stacked objection descends slowly. Type your real rebuttal. 7+ destroys it. 4–6 knocks it back for one more try. 3 or less and it lands.

The pattern you're shooting for: acknowledge the objection, isolate or ask before you answer, reframe to value, then advance the deal. Arguing, caving, and dodging all feel like progress. None of them are.
