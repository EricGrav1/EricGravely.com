How to play

Move

Drag on the screen or use ← → (or A / D) to fly your ship.

Shoot

Tap FIRE or press Space. Ten shots per clip. Big rocks take three hits and pay the most.

Destroy objections

Hit the asteroids before they reach the planet. Clear ten rocks to finish the wave.

Reload with the right answer

Empty clip? A buyer objection appears with four responses. The one that acknowledges and asks refills you. Argue, cave, or dodge and you lose a life.

Boss rocks

After every wave a boss rock descends with a stacked objection. Bullets bounce off. Type your own rebuttal and Sales Coach AI grades it 1–10.

Don't get hit

You have 3 lives. A rock that lands or a wrong reload costs one. Right answers build your heat multiplier.
